# Ultravpn-player 0.7.0

The Android TV interface has been rebuilt for viewing from the sofa and navigating with a remote. A fixed top bar opens Home, Live TV, Movies, Series, My List and Settings without an expanding sidebar taking space from the content.

Live TV now has three independent panels: vertical categories, a channel list, and programme information with an optional compact muted preview. Moving through channels updates the programme panel after a short pause. Press OK to watch the highlighted channel. Left and Right move between panels; Up from the first category or channel returns to navigation. The full TV guide, channel favourites and recently viewed channels remain available.

Movies and Series open directly into large poster grids beside vertical category lists. Search opens only when selected, so remote navigation no longer enters a text cursor accidentally. Sorting, saved categories, provider order/visibility and parental protection remain supported. Home retains Continue Watching, recently added titles and local recommendations based on viewing history.

Movie detail pages have large Play/Resume and My List controls, readable synopsis/cast information and actual provider artwork. Series detail pages separate seasons from episodes, retaining progress, resume prompts and next-episode playback. Settings has its own TV landing page and grouped controls with larger text.

Focused controls use a bright fill and cyan outline; selected categories use a separate teal style. Virtualized lists and grids support held D-pad keys and scroll the next item into view before focusing it. Mobile retains its touch layout. Account information, hidden portal addresses, diagnostics, speed tests and the permanent signing identity are preserved.

The release is version 0.7.0, Android build 17. Use `Ultravpn-player.apk` for the permanent download address. For remote updates, extract `remote-update.zip`, upload both APKs first and replace `update.json` last. Keep older versioned APKs available for users with an in-progress download.
