# Approved television direction

The user approved the Glass guide concept on 23 October 2026. Implementation targets its layout, density, typography, palette and focus hierarchy rather than the earlier large-panel dashboard.

Design units: 1024-wide canvas; 64-high header; 26 horizontal gutter; 129-high Live TV information banner; 136-wide category rail; 16-wide column gap; 122-wide channel identity column; three programme cells; 35-high guide and category rows; 38-high remote hint/footer. Cinema hero: 220 high. Standard shelves: six landscape cards with 10-wide gaps. Inter is bundled locally under its OFL licence. No runtime font service is required.

Programme columns mean On now, Up next and Later. They are ordered programme slots, not a time-proportional timeline. Existing full timeline and catch-up remain in the full guide. No sample title, time or stock photograph is placed in a user's catalogue. Real imagery availability depends on the provider.

Validation includes matching programme/artwork fixtures, 960×540, 1280×720 and 1920×1080 captures, missing artwork/EPG, long categories, held D-pad scrolling through 1500 channels, navigation between columns and future programme dialogs. Existing playback, privacy, protection, updater and mobile checks remain required. Rendered checks do not replace device testing.
